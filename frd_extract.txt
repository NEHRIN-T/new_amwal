Document Overview1.1 PurposeThis Functional Requirements Document (FRD) defines the complete scope, behavior, and user stories for the AMWAL Property Portfolio Management System. Siddiqui) to monitor their real-estate portfolio performance, rental intelligence, occupancy status, and financial projections.BACKEND MANAGEMENT PORTAL ΓÇö An internal tool for the AMWAL operations team (property managers, admins) to enter, manage, and update all data that powers the client dashboard.1.3 Business ContextThe client owns a portfolio of 60 rental properties in Dubai categorized into 4 types:Luxury Villas (LX Villas) ΓÇö 8 unitsStandard Villas ΓÇö 12 unitsLarger Apartments ΓÇö 20 unitsStandard Flats ΓÇö 20 unitsThe client wants visibility into portfolio-level KPIs, rental market gaps, occupancy trends, and financial scenarios ΓÇö all in one place.
